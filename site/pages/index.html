Your own AI agent,
in your pocket.

Hermsy is a private phone app for Hermes Agent, the AI agent you run on your own server. Chat, talk, and let it reach you. No Hermsy account, no Hermsy cloud.

Talk, don't type

Start a voice call and just talk. Hermsy listens and speaks on your phone, waits while you think, and keeps going with the screen off.

Your voice stays on the phone

Download the free voice models once, and speech becomes text on the device. Calls run on the same models.

The Brief

A short summary of your day: what's on, what's due, and what your agent suggests. Also as a home-screen widget.

An Inbox for what your agent sends

Scheduled jobs and reminders arrive as notifications and wait in the Inbox. Reply to any of them in its own chat.

A Chief of Staff

It texts you when something needs you, and stays quiet otherwise. Tell it what matters and it remembers.

Private by design

Locked with a PIN or fingerprint, encrypted on the phone, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Notifications that Google can't read

When your agent sends you something, it stays on your server. Your phone gets a push that carries nothing at all, not even an ID, and then fetches the message from your own Hermes over HTTPS. Google sees only that your phone was woken. How it works.

Up and running in a few minutes

Running Hermes in Docker with hermes-spawn?

  1. hermes-spawn connect <name>: it switches on the API server, installs the Hermsy plugin, gives Hermes an HTTPS address, and shows a QR code.
  2. Install Hermsy and tap Scan QR code.

Any other Hermes works too: see the setup guide.
